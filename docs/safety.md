# Safety notes
- Keep `max_gripper_effort` low; crushed bottles = pills everywhere. Test with empty bottles first.
- Use **empty or candy-filled bottles** at the hackathon — no real medication.
- Clamp joint step size in scripted mode (`max_joint_step_deg`) and keep hands clear when running.
- Have someone on the power switch / Ctrl-C during every demo run.
- SO-101 Feetech servos can overheat holding torque for long periods — torque off between runs.
